Salesdesk privacy policy

Salesdesk is the sales app of Devi Industries. Last updated 12 November 2026.

Who can use the app

Salesdesk is for the sales team of Devi Industries. Only staff with an account created by Devi Industries can sign in. The app does nothing until a staff member signs in with that account.

What the app reads on the phone, and why

  • Call log. The app reads the number, time, length and direction of calls on the SIM cards the staff member chooses. They become call entries on the matching customer in the company CRM, so the team can follow up every enquiry. The app never changes or deletes the call log.
  • Phone state and phone numbers. Used to notice when a business call starts or ends and which SIM it was on, so the call is logged against the right person.
  • Calls. When a staff member taps Call on a customer, in the app or on the CRM website, the app places that call. It never places a call by itself.
  • Contacts (optional). When the staff member turns this on, the app shows the saved contact name for a number that is not yet a customer. Contacts are not uploaded.
  • Call recordings (optional). The app does not record calls. If the phone already saves call recordings, the staff member can choose that folder; the app then uploads the recordings of business calls to the company CRM.
  • Microphone (optional). Used only while the staff member holds the button to record a spoken note about a call.
  • Display over other apps. Shows the customer's name and last note while a business call is ringing.
  • Notifications and running in the background. A notification stays while call logging is on, with a button to pause it.

Where the data goes

Everything the app collects is sent over an encrypted connection to the Devi Industries CRM at the company CRM and nowhere else. It is seen only by Devi Industries staff who have access to the customer.

To write call summaries, uploaded recordings may be sent to speech and AI services that the company uses for this purpose (for example Sarvam AI, OpenAI or Google Gemini). They process the audio only to return a transcript or summary.

The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept

Call entries, recordings and notes are kept for as long as Devi Industries keeps the customer record. Signing out stops all collection on that phone.

Your choices

Contacts, recordings and the microphone are optional and can be turned off in the app's settings at any time. Phone permissions can be removed in Android settings. A staff member, or a customer whose calls were logged, can ask Devi Industries to delete their data by writing to the email address shown on this app's Google Play page.
