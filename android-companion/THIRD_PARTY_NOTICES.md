# Icon attribution

The seven `ic_*.xml` navigation, contact, search and refresh vectors are converted from Lucide 0.468.0 source icons, preserving their original 24-unit geometry and stroke styling.

Source: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/0.468.0/icons
